    Start of Overview

    Overview

    This guidance explains the Sustainable Farming Incentive (SFI) 2023 offer, including who can apply, what actions are available, and how payments are calculated.

    It's aimed at farmers who manage land in England and want to understand how the scheme fits alongside other funding they may already receive.

    Start of Eligibility criteria

    Eligibility criteria

    Land eligibility

    You can apply if you have management control of the land and it is registered on the Rural Payments service.

    Business eligibility

    Applicants must have a valid Single Business Identifier and be actively farming the land included in the application.

    Start of Payment rates

    Payment rates

    Payment rates are published annually and reviewed each scheme year. The current baseline payment is £22 per hectare for the standard soil management action.

    Rates can vary depending on which actions are combined and the total area entered into the scheme.

    Start of Application process

    Application process

    Applications are submitted through the Rural Payments service and assessed in the order they are received .

    You'll need to confirm your land parcels and select the actions you want to include before submitting.

    Start of Supporting evidence

    Supporting evidence

    Keep records of any actions carried out, including dates and photographs where relevant .

    Evidence should be retained for the duration of the agreement in case it is requested during an inspection.

    Start of Review dates

    Review dates

    This guidance is reviewed annually alongside the wider scheme update.

    The next scheduled review is due in the spring , ahead of the following scheme year's application window.
